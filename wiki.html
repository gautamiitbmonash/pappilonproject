Welcome to Pappilon

Pappilon is a free app for women who travel by public transport. You use it to record how places on your journey feel, so researchers can understand what makes them feel safe or unsafe.

Screenshot placeholderThe Pappilon home screen on a phone
Suggested size 800 x 450 px

What you will do

You add the places you pass through on a map, look at a picture of each one, and drop small round pins on whatever you notice: a dark corner, a crowded door, a friendly shopkeeper. You give each pin a score and a short note. When you finish, you send your work to the research team.

What you do not need

  • No technical skills. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use Pappilon.
  • No special phone. Any Android phone with Chrome works.
  • No real name. You sign in with a User ID the project gives you.
VoluntaryTaking part is your choice. You can stop at any time without any consequences.

Words you will meet

Session
One outing or one journey. Everything you record is kept inside a session.
Point or place
A spot on the map you want to describe, such as a bus stop or a crossing.
Panorama
A picture you can look around in, like turning your head on the spot.
Pin
A round marker you place on the picture to describe one thing.
Lens
A way of looking at a place. There are three, and each has its own icons.
Icon
A small symbol for something you can notice, such as light or staring.
Score
A number from 1 (very unsafe) to 5 (very safe).
Tag
A short word that adds detail, such as "dark patch".
Gut check
One last rating for how the whole place feels.
Export
Packing your work into a zip file to share with the team.
Consent
Your agreement to take part, which you give once when you first sign in.
Reminder
A daily nudge to record, if you ask for one.
Using the app

Getting started

Five short steps take you from opening the link to recording your first place. You only do steps 1 to 4 once.

  1. Open the link in ChromeUse the link from your project coordinator. Open it in Chrome itself, not inside WhatsApp or another app.
  2. Add it to your phone (optional)Tap the three-dot menu in Chrome, then Install app. Pappilon now sits on your home screen like any other app.
  3. Create your accountChoose a User ID and a password. Keep them somewhere safe. There is no email recovery.
  4. Read and sign the consent formRead the information sheet, tick each box that you agree with, and tap submit. Your User ID and today's date become your signature. You are asked only once.
  5. Start or open a sessionGive your outing a name, such as "Tuesday morning bus". The map opens and you are ready.

Using the app

A tour of the screen

The main screen has four parts. Learn where they are and you will never feel lost.

Screenshot placeholderThe main screen with the four parts numbered
Suggested size 800 x 480 px

Top bar

Shows your saving status, your level, and buttons for Reminder, Insights, and Finish and export.

Map and list

The map shows your places as dots. The list names each one. Tap either to open a place.

Picture panel

The picture of the selected place. Look around, then tap it to drop a pin.

Tag tab

Where you choose a lens, pick an icon, give a score, add tags and a note.

What the dots mean

  • A plain dot is a place you have added but not finished.
  • A finished dot is a place you have closed with a gut check.
  • The place you are working on is highlighted.
Saved"Saved" in the top bar means your work is safely in your account. "Offline, will retry" means your phone has no internet. Keep working; it sends later.
Stage 1 · Add

Adding places

A place is any spot on your journey you want to describe. You can add one by tapping the map, or by pressing a button while you are standing there.

Add a place from the map

  1. Zoom the map to where you want the place.
  2. Tap the spot. A new place appears in the list and on the map.
  3. Give it a short name you will recognise, such as "Station subway".

Add a place where you stand

Tap REPORT. The app uses your phone's location to add a place right where you are. Allow location when your phone asks.

Screenshot placeholderThe map with a new place and the REPORT button
Suggested size 800 x 420 px

Add many places at once

If your coordinator gave you a list of places as a CSV file, use Import to bring them all in together.

Delete a place

Touch and hold its row in the list, then confirm Delete.

The 500 m ruleDo not record places within 500 metres of your home or workplace.
Stage 2 · Look

Pictures of places

Each place needs a picture to pin things onto. Pappilon offers three sources.

Street View

For outdoor places the app finds the nearest Street View picture automatically.

Standard interiors

For journeys inside a bus, metro or local train, choose a standard Mumbai interior.

Your own picture

Load a photo you took, if it shows no faces and no number plates.

Screenshot placeholderThe picture panel with the three source buttons
Suggested size 800 x 400 px

If there is no Street View

The app looks within 120 metres. If it finds nothing, tap Search 400 m, or choose a standard interior or your own image.

Looking around

Drag the picture to turn. Pinch or scroll to zoom. Flat photos can be dragged and zoomed too.

Heads-upPictures stay on the phone where you loaded them. Use the same phone to finish and export.
Stage 3 · Pin

Pin what you notice

This is the heart of the app. A pin is a small round marker you place on the picture, then describe. Add as many as you like, one for each thing you notice.

  1. Choose a lensOpen the Tag tab. Three buttons at the top are lenses, which are different ways of looking. See The three lenses.
  2. Pick an iconTap the icon for what you want to describe, such as Light or Staring. On a computer, hover to read a short explanation.
  3. Tap the pictureA round pin lands where you tap. A dashed ring means you have not rated it yet.
  4. Give it a scoreTap one of five faces to say how safe that thing makes the place feel.
    1 Very unsafe2 Unsafe3 Mixed4 Safe5 Very safe
  5. Add tags and a noteTap any tags that fit, or type your own in the + tag box. Then write a note in your own words, such as "shutters down after 7pm, nowhere to sit".
  6. Tap "Done with this pin"Your pin is saved. Tap the picture again to add the next one.
Screenshot placeholderA picture with several pins and the Tag tab beside it
Suggested size 800 x 480 px

Fix or remove a pin

  • Move it: press and drag the pin on the picture.
  • Change it: tap the pin, then pick a different icon or score.
  • Delete it: tap the pin, then the bin button.

When no icon fits

Choose Other, describe it and write what is happening in your own words. No score is needed for this one.

Plain wordsObserve first. Describe what you can see or hear before guessing why someone acted that way. Mark any guess as your interpretation.
Reference

The three lenses and their icons

A lens is a way of looking at a place. Each lens has its own icons. Use whichever icon matches what you notice. You can switch lenses at any time, even for pins on the same picture.

Lens 1

Harassment and unwanted attention

Unwanted behaviour towards women and girls.

  • Staring or watching
  • Catcalling or comments
  • Sexual gestures
  • Following or trailing
  • Blocking or trapping
  • Unwanted closeness
  • Unwanted touching
  • Unwanted photographing
  • Sexual exposure
  • Threat or intimidation
  • Sexual or physical assault
  • Repeated harassment
  • Response or help
  • Effect on movement
  • Other, describe it
Lens 2

Street conditions

The surroundings that can make a place feel safer or less safe.

  • Places to stay
  • People around
  • See and be seen
  • Light
  • Easy to move
  • Ways out and help
  • Clean and cared for
  • Feel welcome
  • Useful facilities
  • Active edges (shops, vendors)
  • Comfort and pleasure
  • Other, describe it
Lens 3

Human behaviour and social response

What people nearby do and say. Record what is visible, not guesses about why.

  • Peer or group response
  • Bystander response
  • Target response or impact
  • Accountability or deterrence
  • Social cues and norms
  • Situation or opportunity
  • Interaction sequence
  • Interpretation, describe it

A worked example

  1. You walk through a dim underpass. Open Street conditions.
  2. Tap the Light icon, then tap the dark spot on the picture.
  3. Score it 2, Unsafe. Tag it Dark patch and Broken light.
  4. Note: "Two lamps are out, I walked faster here."

Stages 4 and 5 · Settle and send off

Finish a place, then send it off

When you have pinned what you want to say about a place, you close it with one last question. When your outing is over, you pack everything up to share.

Settle: finish the place

  1. Do the gut checkAt the bottom of the Tag tab, tap the face that matches how safe the whole place feels, from 1 (very unsafe) to 5 (very safe).
  2. Tap "Complete point and next"The place turns into a finished dot on the map, you earn points, and the app takes you to your next unfinished place.
  3. Changed your mind?Open the place and tap Reopen this point. You can then add pins or change ratings.
Screenshot placeholderGut check faces and the Complete button
Suggested size 800 x 400 px

Levels and Insights

Pins, tags, notes and finished places earn points that move you up the levels in the top bar: Curious Walker, Street Scout, Sightline Sleuth, Lantern Bearer, City Cartographer and Right-to-Loiter Legend. It is only friendly encouragement. The Insights button shows which themes you rated lowest, the tags you used most, and the places that felt least safe to you.

Send off: export your work

  1. Tap Finish and export in the top bar.
  2. Tap Build my .zip. The app draws your pins onto each picture and packs a zip file with the annotated pictures, a list of every pin and a list of every place.
  3. Choose how to share: Share (WhatsApp, email and more on a phone), Download, Email, WhatsApp, or a private link that works for 7 days.
  4. Send it to whoever your project coordinator told you to.

Good habits

Daily reminders

A small daily nudge can help you remember to record.

  1. Tap the bell button, Reminder, in the top bar.
  2. Choose the time you would like to be reminded.
  3. Tap Turn on reminder. If your phone asks about notifications, tap Allow.
  4. Tap Send test to check it works. Turn it off any time from the same window.
Screenshot placeholderThe Daily reminder window
Suggested size 500 x 470 px

If you have not recorded anything that day (a place, a pin, a rating or a finished place), you get a nudge after your chosen time, and a prompt when you open the app. Once you record something, it stays quiet until tomorrow.

Heads-upReminders work best when Pappilon is installed on your phone. If the app has been closed for a long time, Android may delay or skip one. If notifications are blocked, allow them in Chrome's site settings.
Good habits

Staying safe and private

Just as important as recording is keeping yourself and your privacy safe while you take part.

Your safety comes first

Use the app only when it is safe to. Stop walking or step aside before you record. If you feel unsafe, put the phone away.

The 500 m rule

Do not record places within 500 metres of your home or workplace.

No identifying details

Do not write names, phone numbers or number plates, and do not use photos that show faces.

Record only what is safe to document

For serious incidents, note only what is appropriate and safe. You never have to describe more than you want to.

It is your choice

Taking part is voluntary. You can stop at any time without any consequences.

Not an emergency tool

Pappilon does not alert anyone. In an emergency call 112. Women's helpline: 181. Railway helpline: 139.

Help

When something does not go to plan

Most problems have a quick fix. The question-mark button in the top bar also opens a short built-in help.

I forgot my password.
Ask your project coordinator to reset it. There is no email recovery, so keep your User ID somewhere safe.
The top bar says "Offline, will retry".
Your phone has no internet right now. Keep working. The app sends everything when the connection returns.
The picture is missing on a new phone.
Pictures stay on the phone where you loaded them. Open the place and load the picture again. Your pins are safe in your account.
It says "No Street View within 120 m".
Tap Search 400 m, or choose a standard bus, metro or train interior, or one of your own images.
Nothing happens when I tap the picture.
Wait for the picture to finish loading and make sure a place is selected in the list.
The REPORT button cannot find me.
Allow location when your phone asks. If you said no before, allow it in Chrome's site settings, and make sure your phone's location is switched on.
I cannot install the app.
Open the link in Chrome itself, not inside another app. Reload the page once, then use the three-dot menu and tap Install app.
I added a place by mistake.
Touch and hold its row in the list, then confirm Delete.
I dropped a pin by accident.
Tap the pin, then tap the bin button. On a computer you can also press Del.
Where is my work?
"Saved" in the top bar means it is in your account. On another device, log in with the same User ID and open the same session name.
