About the project

Small wings, big change.

Public transport gets millions of women to work, school and home. Yet the stops, stairs, platforms and compartments they pass through are rarely designed with their comfort in mind.

Pappilon is a research app that turns daily travel into data. Participants add places, look at a picture of each one, and pin what they notice: the light, the crowd, the stares, the help that was there. Researchers read these pins across hundreds of places and share what they learn.

Made for commutersWorks on a phone, in a few taps, between stops.
Private by designNo real names. You choose what to share and can stop any time.
Free and openDedicated to the public domain under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0).
How it works

From a ride to a map in five stages.

Like a butterfly, every outing goes through the same stages. Tap each one.

Add the places on your journey.

Tap the map or press REPORT where you stand. A bus stop, a crossing, a station subway: any spot you want to describe becomes a place in your list.

Screenshot placeholderThe map with places added
Suggested size 640 x 400 px

Look around the picture.

Each place comes with a picture from Street View, a standard bus, metro or train interior, or a photo of your own. Drag to look around, like turning your head.

Screenshot placeholderThe picture panel with a panorama
Suggested size 640 x 400 px

Pin what you notice.

Tap the picture to drop a pin. Choose an icon, give a score from 1 to 5, and add a few words. A dim underpass, a helpful vendor, a crowded door: each gets its own pin.

Settle the place with one rating.

When you have said what you wanted, answer one question: how safe does the whole place feel? Then move on to the next one, and watch your level grow.

Send your work off.

Pack everything into one zip with your annotated pictures and lists. Share it by WhatsApp, email or a private link, and your observations take flight.

Three lenses

Three ways of looking at one place.

Safety is more than one thing. Pappilon gives you three lenses, each with its own set of icons.

Lens 1

Harassment and unwanted attention

Staring, comments, following, touching and how people responded.

Lens 2

Street conditions

Light, crowds, clear sightlines, places to sit, ways out and help.

Lens 3

Human behaviour

What people nearby do and say, and how bystanders react.

Built with care

Your safety and privacy come first.

Informed consent

You read the information sheet and agree once, before anything is recorded.

Voluntary

Take part when you like and stop at any time without any consequences.

No identifying details

No names, numbers or faces. Places within 500 m of your home or work are not recorded.

Not an emergency tool

Pappilon does not alert anyone. In an emergency call 112.
